      What-If Scenario Builder

      Compare two customer profiles side-by-side

      Fill out the sidebar form with Profile A values, score it, then change values and score again. The results panel shows the delta.

      1Fill the sidebar form with your baseline customer profile.
      2Click "Analyze churn risk" to score Profile A.
      3Change one or more fields (e.g. switch contract type, add a service).
      4Score again — the dashboard shows the before vs after delta.

      Tips

      High-impact changes

      • Contract type is typically the #1 churn driver — try switching between monthly and annual.
      • Adding support services (backup, security) often reduces risk by 15–30%.
      • Tenure interacts with everything — short-tenured customers are inherently riskier.
      • High monthly charges compound risk, especially with month-to-month contracts.

      Interpreting Results

      Reading the risk factors

      • Red bars = features pushing churn probability up.
      • Green bars = features pushing churn probability down.
      • The wider the bar, the stronger that feature's influence on the prediction.
      • Click any factor to see a detailed impact breakdown.

      Training Guide

      How to improve your model

      📋 Data Quality

      Ensure your CSV has consistent column names and no more than 5% missing values. The system auto-fills blanks, but clean data trains better models.

      ⚖️ Class Balance

      Aim for at least 15% churn rate in your training data. If your data is very imbalanced, the model uses balanced class weights to compensate.

      📊 Volume

      More data = better predictions. We recommend at least 500 rows for a reliable model. Each upload adds to your cumulative training set.

      🔄 Retraining

      After uploading new data, ask your platform admin to run the retraining pipeline. Models are versioned — the old one stays until the new one is ready.
